Overleaf Re-design

AI-powered companion that helps you discover places

Overview

Overleaf is a Web-based LaTex editor that is used to create, collaborate, edit, and share documents. It is usuallly used for academic papers which contain high-level mathematical concepts and formulas.

My Role

I conducted user interviews and preliminary research, led ideation for collaboration and AI features to improve suggestions and the AI flow, and prototyped using Figma.

Problem

User Interview

We conducted qualitative in-depth interview with 6 interviewees. Given a limited time-frame, we kept interviews relatively open-ended. Participants shared their screen and walked through how they use the platform.
We asked questions regarding frequency of using platform, typical uses, challanegs with the platform, and compares with competitors.
