User Persona

User Persona Section
JORDAN
26 years old
Graphic Designer
Brooklyn, NY

Needs

  • Quickly find affordable office furniture and design tools.
  • Sell used equipment and products to minimize clutter.
  • Navigate the platform easily without distractions.

Motivation

Save time buying and selling. Has used classifieds for years but feels current designs slow the process; wants a more efficient flow.

RACHEL
22 years old
Undergrad Student
Manhattan, NY

Needs

  • Find affordable secondhand textbooks, furniture, and clothes.
  • Sell items she no longer needs for quick cash.
  • Quickly assess listing legitimacy and trustworthiness.

Motivation

Wants a more efficient, user‑friendly experience that helps her find what she needs quickly while keeping transactions safe and trustworthy.
