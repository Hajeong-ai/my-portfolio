APP LAUNCH · SUMMER 2025

🔍 Launching an AI-powered mobile app that helps users to achieve daily wellness goals

Routine Out Loud

  • 🏆 Selected for the Pre-Startup Package, awarded government funding of 30M KRW

    • Overview

    Nowadays, people often set ambitious wellness goals but lose motivation along the way. The reason for this is because of the pressure to meet ideal standards amplified by constant social comparison, turns what should be a journey of self-care into a source of stress and discouragement.

    Solution is ROL. ROL is a mobile routine app that allows users to build daily routine and achieve wellness goals. ROL helps users to create, track, and maintain habits for healthier and more productive life.

    My Role

    I was responsible for 0 to 1 product design and launch, including design planning, content strategy, prototyping, app store screenshot creation, social media marketing, and leading the app launch with iterative testing and QA.

    User Interview

    Before designing ROL, we wanted to understand user's emotional and behavioral patterns behind maintaining daily golas, especially what factors make them discouraged or motivated.

    Through the interview, we focused on understanding how often participants set daily goals, what types of goals they usually set, and how frequently they felt discouraged when trying to achieve them, as well as genral questions about their daily life.

    Semi-structured interview
    Number of Interviewees: 12

    Problem

    🎯 Goal Challenges

    73% of users abandoned their goals within 2 weeks

    🤝 Lack of connection

    Users wanted to join a community of people who share similar goals

    🎨 Lack of Personalization

    Users lost motivation to achieve their goals because those goals were tailored to social norms, which perfectly match their interests

    How might we create a sense of community that motivates users to maintain their wellness routines?

    Competitive Analysis

    We conducted a competitive analysis to come up with the idea that can differenciate ROL in the wellness mobile app market.

    Routinary
    • 5M+ users, Personal routine timer, strong global scalability
    • No real time interaction
    • Revenue model: subscripton focused
    Challangers
    • 1M users, strong habits formation based on monetary incentives
    • No real-time interaction, domestic focused
    • Revenue model: event participation fee
    Habtify
    • 3M+ users, data driven strategic habit management, strong global scalability
    • No real-time interaction, domestic focused, data driven personalized recommendation features
    • Revenue model: subscripton
    Habitica
    • 4M+ users, gamified habit management, strong global PRG
    • No real-time interaction, domestic focused, no personalized recommendation feature
    • Revenue model: in-app purchase
    My Routine
    • 1.5M+ users, social based routine management
    • No real-time interaction, domestic focused (South Korea), personalized recommendation based on templete
    • Revenue model: free focused

    Research to Ideation

    After finishing the competitive analysis, our team came up with the three key features that that can differenciate ROL; AI feature, community, and global conection.

    Rolly (AI)
    🤖

    Accurately identify the wellness that fits user's body and mind, recommend the most relevant content, and deliver it in a clear, easy-to-digest summary.

    Community
    🧑‍🤝‍🧑

    A comfortable space not overwhelmed by mixed information and promotions, but deeply relatable content and meaningful connections.

    Global Connection
    🌐

    Not just simple connections, but a link across time and space — a single, ever-evolving space that stays active even while users are in different time zone.

    Problem

    🔧 Autofix and Suggestion

    Split across multiple flows, making it hard to locate errors or the right suggestions

    🤝 Collaboration

    Needs more clarity on parts of documents which have been previously edited and who has ownership on different parts

    📚 Citation

    Current citation process is complex and monotonous, and users need to input manually

    Defining Design System

    Establishing a design system in early stage was important, a well-defined and consistent system will result in minimizing the communication gaps between developers during the development process.

    I chose Noto Sans, which is a global font collection that has modern vibe (Google Fonts), that matches the app's goal to reach out and connect people around the world.

    For the color scheme, I chose blue as the primary color as the competitor analysis showed most wellness apps use warm tones, creating differentiation opportunity.

    Design

    01

    Splash & Onboarding

    I worked on the splash image and sign-up flow. In the process, users agree to the terms and conditions, then provide personal information to complete account creation.

    02

    Adding Routine (home)

    From the home page, users can tap the + button in the bottom right corner to add a new routine. In this page, users can input the routine name, select a list and category, set notifications, and attach images and descriptions.

    03

    My Room (Community)

    My Room serves as an ecosystem-driven community that encourages users to share their wellness activities and build meaningful connections. Within My Room, users can explore other users' profiles to discover which rooms they belong to, which routines they follow and like, and what posts they have created.

    04

    Rolly (ROL AI)

    Rolly (ROL AI) is an AI-driven wellness platform that transforms user input into personalized routines and communities.
    By combining LLMs with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and leveraging large-scale wellness and biobank datasets, Rolly interprets users’ concerns and generates wellness insights. These insights are then translated into actionable routines, where users can share activities, join supportive communities, and sustain long-term motivation.

    05

    Payment Page

    On the payment page, users can subscribe for a VIP pass, unlocking the ability to add routines per purchase.

    My Impact

    1. By defining a consistent design system during the early stage of the project, we established shared visual and interaction guidelines that streamlined communication between designers and developers. This not only ensured design consistency across screens but also enabled developers to implement components more efficiently, reducing rework and development time.

    2. Through user test conducted using Figma prototype, allowed our team to find the errors and improvements during the iteration and QA process. By conducting multiple rounds of user testing, we were able to achieve a 100% task completion in usability testing (n=12)

    3. Pre-Startup Package Winner: 30M KRW government funding
