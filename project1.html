ROL

AI-powered mobile app that helps users to achieve daily wellness goals

🚀 Selected for the Pre-Startup Package, awarded government funding of 30M KRW

Overview

ROL is a mobile routine app that allows users to build daily routine and achieve wellness goals. ROL helps users to create, track, and maintain habits for healthier and more productive life.

My Role

I was responsible for 0 to 1 product design and launch, including design planning, content strategy, prototyping, app store screenshot creation, social media marketing, and leading the app launch with iterative testing and QA.

Problem

Nowadays, people often set ambitious wellness goals but lose motivation along the way.
The reason for this is because of the pressure to meet ideal standards amplified by constant social comparison, turns what should be a journey of self-care into a source of stress and discouragement.

🧠
71%

18% of office workers give up on wellness activities within the first week, 20% within two weeks, and 11% within three weeks — in total, 71.3% give up within one month.

😵‍💫
90%

Over 90% of community members are observers, often experiencing a lack of belonging and increased feelings of loneliness. About 68% stop participating after their first post.

Competitive Benchmarking

We conducted competitive benchmarking to explore how we could design an app that helps people achieve their wellness goals.

Routinary
  • 5M+ users, Personal routine timer, strong global scalability
  • No real time interaction
  • Revenue model: subscripton focused
Challangers
  • 1M users, strong habits formation based on monetary incentives
  • No real-time interaction, domestic focused
  • Revenue model: event participation fee
Habtify
  • 3M+ users, data driven strategic habit management, strong global scalability
  • No real-time interaction, domestic focused, data driven personalized recommendation features
  • Revenue model: subscripton
Habitica
  • 4M+ users, gamified habit management, strong global PRG
  • No real-time interaction, domestic focused, no personalized recommendation feature
  • Revenue model: in-app purchase
My Routine
  • 1.5M+ users, social based routine management
  • No real-time interaction, domestic focused (South Korea), personalized recommendation based on templete
  • Revenue model: free focused

Research to Solution

After finishing the competitive analysis, our team was able to come up with the strength of ROL, that can differenciate with other routine management platforms; AI feature, community, and global conection.

Rolly (AI)
🤖

Accurately identify the wellness that fits user's body and mind, recommend the most relevant content, and deliver it in a clear, easy-to-digest summary.

Community
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

A comfortable space not overwhelmed by mixed information and promotions, but deeply relatable content and meaningful connections.

Global Connection
🌐

Not just simple connections, but a link across time and space — a single, ever-evolving space that stays active even while users are in different time zone.

Defining Design System

Establishing a design system in early stage was important, since I thought a well-defined and consistent system will result in minimizing the communication gaps between developers during the development process.

Key Features

01

Splash & Onboarding

I worked on the splash image and sign-up flow. In the process, users agree to the terms and conditions, then provide personal information to complete account creation.

02

Adding Routine (home)

From the home page, users can tap the + button in the bottom right corner to add a new routine. In this page, users can input the routine name, select a list and category, set notifications, and attach images and descriptions.

03

My Room (Community)

My Room serves as an ecosystem-driven community that encourages users to share their wellness activities and build meaningful connections. Within My Room, users can explore other users' profiles to discover which rooms they belong to, which routines they follow and like, and what posts they have created.

04

Rolly (ROL AI)

Rolly (ROL AI) is an AI-driven wellness platform that transforms user input into personalized routines and communities.
By combining LLMs with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and leveraging large-scale wellness and biobank datasets, Rolly interprets users’ concerns and generates wellness insights. These insights are then translated into actionable routines, where users can share activities, join supportive communities, and sustain long-term motivation.

05

Payment Page

On the payment page, users can subscribe for a VIP pass, unlocking the ability to add routines per purchase.

Impact

1. By defining a consistent design system during the early stage of the project, we established shared visual and interaction guidelines that streamlined communication between designers and developers. This not only ensured design consistency across screens but also enabled developers to implement components more efficiently, reducing rework and development time.

2. Through user test conducted using Figma prototype, allowed our team to find the errors and improvements during the iteration and QA process. By conducting multiple rounds of user testing, we were able to achieve a 100% success rate in app usage.

What did I learn?

1. Collaborating with front and backend developers and project manager, I learned that effective communication greatly influences the speed of design implementation especially for the early stage start-up, and during that stage, cross functional feedback is essential.

2. In an early-stage startup marked by rapid decision execution and high autonomy, I learned to manage ambiguity and uncertainty effectively.

3. The experience of refelcting iteration durtin the iteration process gave me a lesson of how I should priortize feedback.
