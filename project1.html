Problem

🧠
71%

18% of office workers give up on wellness activities within the first week, 20% within two weeks, and 11% within three weeks — in total, 71.3% give up within one month.

😵‍💫
90%

Over 90% of community members are lurkers (observers), often experiencing a lack of belonging and increased feelings of loneliness — about 68% stop participating after their first post.

Research

By researching and comparing, we were able to Find how ROL will be differenciated with other wellness services.

As a result, we discovered our strength comes from global wellness ecosystem based on real-time communication, which leverages time zone difference.
