Living in Transition... 🇰🇷 🇺🇸 🇦🇹

I was born in South Korea and later moved to United States for study. After high school, I studied independently in Berkeley and Los Angeles, where living alone cultivated resilience and independence. This experience gave me deeper empathy for the diverse challenges people navigate in their daily lives. Every changes in my life was a meaningful step that shaped me to become a designer who values diversity.

🎓 I studied International Development Studies with a minor in Digital Humanities at UCLA, where I gained a strong theoretical foundation for understanding user needs in diverse global contexts. This summer, I worked as a product designer at a startup, where I launched a mobile routine app, starting from 0 to 1, and took part in every step of the design process.

Reach out to me if you have an idea that can make people's lives better, I'm happy to collaborate!
