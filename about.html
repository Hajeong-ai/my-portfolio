Living in Transition...

I was born in South Korea and later moved to United States, where experiencing different cultures broadened my perspective. After high school, I studied independently in Berkeley and Los Angeles, where living alone taught me resilience and independence. More recently, I immersed myself in new cultural differences, each change becoming a meaningful step that shaped me into a designer who values diversity and the challenges people face. Now, in Seattle, I am opening a new chapter of my life with curiosity and determination.

I earned my degree in International Development Studies with a minor in Digital Humanities at UCLA. This summer, I worked as a product designer at a startup, where I launched a mobile routine app and took part in every step of the design process—from wireframing and prototyping to user testing and branding. Through this journey, I learned the value of design thinking and creating user-centered experiences. Enjoy exploring my website, and feel free to reach out!
